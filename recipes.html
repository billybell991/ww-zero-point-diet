🍳 Spinach & Tomato Egg Scramble

Yields: 1 supper portion + lunches
Pro Tip: To ensure a prepped scramble doesn’t turn into a watery, spongy mess when reheated for lunches later in the week, you must sweat the moisture out of the tomatoes first.

Ingredients

  • 12 Whole eggs (whisked well)
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, seeded and diced
  • 2 huge handfuls Fresh baby spinach
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic powder
  • To taste: Coarse salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • As needed: Zero-point non-stick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Sweat the Tomatoes: Spray a large non-stick skillet with cooking spray and place over medium-high heat. Add the diced tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until they soften and the excess juice in the pan completely evaporates.
  2. Wilt the Greens: Reduce heat to medium. Drop the fresh spinach directly into the pan with the tomatoes and toss for about 60 seconds, just until it collapses. Transfer the tomato/spinach mix to a clean plate and set aside.
  3. Cook Low and Slow: Wipe the skillet clean, give it another light mist of cooking spray, and set the heat to medium-low. Pour in your whisked eggs. Let them sit for a moment to set slightly on the bottom, then use a rubber spatula to gently push the eggs across the pan, forming soft, tender curds.
  4. Fold and Cool: Just before the eggs are completely set (while they still look slightly wet and glossy), fold the spinach and tomatoes back in, along with the garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Stir for 30 seconds to combine, then immediately remove from heat.
  5. Storage: Enjoy your portion for supper now. For the lunch prep, let the remaining scramble cool completely on the counter before putting the lids on your storage containers. This stops condensation from making the eggs rubbery in the fridge.

🥗 Marinated Mushroom Salad

Yields: Prep-friendly lunch sides
Pro Tip: To prevent soggy greens, marinate the hearty veggies in the dressing and store them together. Pack your delicate greens separately and toss them in just before you eat.

The Dressing

Whisk these together in a small bowl or jar until emulsified:

  • 3 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard (check the label to ensure 0g sugar)
  • Juice of 1/2 Lemon
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic powder
  • To taste: Coarse salt & freshly cracked black pepper

The Salad Components

  • 8–10 oz White button or cremini mushrooms, sliced thin
  • 2-3 stalks Celery, diced or thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup Red onion, sliced into thin slivers
  • 4 cups Arugula or baby spinach (kept separate until serving)

Instructions

  1. Marinate: In a medium bowl, toss the sliced mushrooms, celery, and red onions directly into the dressing. Let it sit at room temperature for 15 minutes. The acid will soften the mushrooms and take the raw, sharp edge off the onions.
  2. Portion: Divide the marinated mushroom mix evenly into your lunch containers.
  3. Pack the Greens: Keep your arugula or spinach in separate baggies or compartments. When lunch rolls around, just drop the fresh greens into the marinated mix and toss!

🍲 Heavy-Hitting Beef, Potato & Cabbage Stew

Yields: 4 suppers
Pro Tip: Dropping cabbage straight into the stew is an elite-level move. It melts right into the savory beef broth, soaking up flavor and boosting volume for a flat 0 points.

Ingredients

  • Enough for 4 bowls Lean beef stew meat cubes (eye of round or top sirloin)
  • 1/2 Large Spanish onion (chopped)
  • 1 bag Yellow potatoes (cubed)
  • As needed Celery and carrots (sliced)
  • 1/2 head Cabbage (chopped into thick ribbons)
  • 1 large carton Low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Fine table salt (or 1.5 tsp Kosher salt)
  • 1-1.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 1-1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1-1.5 tsp Dried Italian seasoning
  • As needed Zero-point non-stick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Sear the Beef: Spray a large pot with zero-point cooking spray. Sear the beef stew cubes until browned on all sides. Season heavily with the measured salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Aromatics: Toss in the chopped Spanish onion, celery, and carrots. Cook for 3–4 minutes until they begin to soften.
  3. The Broth: Pour in the full carton of beef broth and the Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a boil, then drop to a low simmer, cover, and let it ride for 45 minutes to tenderize the beef.
  4. The Volume: Stir in your cubed yellow potatoes and thick cabbage ribbons. (The cabbage will wilt down into the broth, mimicking thick, savory noodles).
  5. Simmer & Portion: Cover and simmer for 30 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender and the stew is thick. Divide evenly into 4 supper containers and snap the lids down.

🍔 Pre-Packed Burger Bowls

Yields: 4 lunches
Pro Tip: Pack the lettuce, pickles, and sauce separately if you plan to microwave the beef portion at lunchtime.

Ingredients

  • Enough for 4 bowls Lean ground beef
  • 1 head Iceberg lettuce (shredded)
  • 1 jar Pickles (diced)
  • 1 tsp Fine table salt (or 1.5 tsp Kosher salt)
  • 1-1.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 1-1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1-1.5 tsp Onion powder
  • 4 portions Marinated Button Mushroom Salad

The "Secret Sauce" (1 Point per batch)

  • 1 cup Fat-free cottage cheese
  • 4 tbsp Low-sugar ketchup
  • Splash Pickle juice
  • Pinch Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. The Sauce: Blend the cottage cheese with the ketchup, pickle juice, and a pinch of garlic powder until silky smooth to mimic real burger spread. Set aside.
  2. Brown the Beef: Brown the ground beef in a skillet with the measured salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Drain any excess fat.
  3. The Base: Line up 4 lunch containers and pack a dense bed of shredded iceberg lettuce into the bottom of each.
  4. The Protein & Toppings: Divide the cooked beef evenly on top of the lettuce. Add the diced pickles and split the Marinated Button Mushroom Salad evenly among the 4 bowls.
  5. The Finish: Drizzle the smooth cottage cheese sauce over the top (or pack separately if heating the beef later), seal the lids, and stack them in the fridge.

🥧 Zero-Point Shepherd's Pie

Yields: 4 suppers
Pro Tip: Since potatoes are good to go on WW, we're making a real, hearty potato mash! Blending in fat-free cottage cheese instead of butter and cream keeps it at zero points while maintaining a rich, silky texture.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 99% fat-free ground turkey (or extra-lean chicken)
  • 1 medium Onion, diced
  • 1 bag (16oz) Frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn, green beans)
  • 1 cup Low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1.5 - 2 lbs Yellow or Russet potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  • 1/2 cup Fat-free cottage cheese (blended until smooth)
  • 1 tsp Fine table salt (divided)
  • 1 tsp Black pepper (divided)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • As needed Zero-point non-stick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. The Zero-Point Mash: Boil the cubed potatoes until fork-tender. Drain well and return to the warm pot. Add the blended cottage cheese, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, and 1/2 tsp garlic powder. Mash thoroughly until silky and thick.
  2. The Savory Base: Spray a large skillet with cooking spray over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey and diced onion. Cook until the turkey is browned and the onions are soft.
  3. The Flavor & Veggies: Stir in the remaining 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, remaining garlic powder, and thyme. Add the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, and beef broth, followed by the frozen mixed vegetables. Simmer for about 5-8 minutes until the liquid reduces and thickens into a light gravy.
  4. Assembly & Bake: Pour the meat and vegetable filling into a 9x9 baking dish (or directly into 4 oven-safe glass meal prep containers). Spread the creamy potato mash evenly over the top.
  5. The Golden Finish: Bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until the edges are bubbling and the potatoes start to get golden spots. Let cool completely before snapping the lids on for your 4 suppers!

🍋 Sheet Pan Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Sweet Potato Hash

Yields: 4 lunches
Pro Tip: Adapted from your Keto Sheet Pan Shrimp but optimized for WW by swapping in hearty sweet potatoes! Roast the sweet potatoes first since they take longer, then toss the shrimp on at the very end so they stay juicy and tender.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 - 2 lbs Large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2-3 medium Sweet potatoes, washed and cubed
  • 2 Bell peppers (any color), chopped
  • 1 small Red onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 Lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • To taste: Coarse salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • As needed: Zero-point non-stick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Roast the Hash: Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper or foil and spray with non-stick spray. Spread the cubed sweet potatoes, bell peppers, and red onions evenly. Spray the top lightly, season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Roast for 20-25 minutes until the sweet potatoes are tender.
  2. Prep the Shrimp: While the veggies roast, toss the shrimp in a bowl with the minced garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. The Quick Finish: Pull the sheet pan out of the oven. Push the veggies slightly to the side or scatter the shrimp directly on top. Return to the oven for 5–7 minutes, just until the shrimp turn pink and opaque.
  4. Portion: Let cool, then divide the shrimp and sweet potato hash into 4 lunch containers. Pack an apple and a couple of hard-boiled eggs on the side for your daily staples!

🍗 "Melt-In-Your-Mouth" Chicken & Fiesta Bean Salad

Yields: 4 suppers
Pro Tip: Inspired by the Melt-In-Your-Mouth Chicken and Mexican Black Bean Salad from your site! Using fat-free Greek yogurt (or blended cottage cheese) as the chicken coating keeps it incredibly moist and completely zero-point friendly.

The Chicken Ingredients

  • 4 Boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup Plain fat-free Greek yogurt (or smooth fat-free cottage cheese)
  • 1/4 cup Grated parmesan cheese (the only points in the dish, very low when divided by 4!)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp Onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp Seasoned salt & black pepper

The Fiesta Bean Salad Ingredients

  • 1 can (15oz) Black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (15oz) Sweet corn, drained
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup Red onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Juice of 1 Lime
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin

Instructions

  1. The Fiesta Salad: In a large mixing bowl, combine the black beans, corn, tomatoes, red onion, and cilantro. Pour over the lime juice, add the cumin, salt, and pepper. Toss well and store in the fridge to let the flavors mingle.
  2. Prep the Chicken: Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a baking dish with zero-point cooking spray. Place the chicken breasts in the dish.
  3. The "Melt in Your Mouth" Coating: In a small bowl, mix the Greek yogurt (or cottage cheese), parmesan, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Smear this mixture evenly over the top of each chicken breast.
  4. Bake: Bake uncovered for 30–35 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (165°F internally) and the yogurt topping has formed a flavorful, savory crust.
  5. Portion: Divide the Fiesta Bean Salad among 4 supper containers and top each with a Melt-In-Your-Mouth Chicken Breast.
