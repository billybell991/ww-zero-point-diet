Zero-Point Master Recipes

🥣 Maple Cinnamon Oatmeal

A warm, filing, and perfectly sweet morning classic.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Rolled Oatmeal
  • 3/4 cup Water
  • Small shot of Maple Syrup
  • Sprinkle of Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Combine oatmeal and water in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Microwave on High for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in maple syrup and top with a sprinkle of cinnamon.

🍍 Tropical Fruit Bowl

Sweet, creamy, high-protein treat to end the day.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Fat-free Cottage Cheese (or Fat-free Greek Yogurt)
  • 1/2 cup diced Pineapple
  • 1 whole Apple (diced)

Instructions

  1. Add cottage cheese or greek yogurt to a bowl.
  2. Top with chopped pineapple and diced apple. Enjoy cold.

🥗 Southwest Chicken Bowl

A fast, fresh kick of lime and zero-point salsa.

Ingredients

  • Shredded rotisserie chicken (skinless breast)
  • 1 can Black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 can Sweet corn (drained)
  • Fat-free salsa
  • Juice of 1 lime & fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. Combine bounds of chicken, beans, and corn in 4 prep bowls.
  2. Top generously with fat-free salsa.
  3. Squeeze lime over top and garnish with cilantro.

🍲 Heavy Red Turkey Chili

Rich, thick, and fully zero point.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 99% fat-free ground turkey
  • 1 Large onion, diced
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes (or crushed)
  • 2 cans Kidney beans, 1 can Black beans
  • 3 tbsp Chili powder, 1 tbsp Cumin, 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 cup beef or chicken broth

Instructions

  1. Brown turkey and onions.
  2. Add spices and stir until fragrant (1 min).
  3. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth.
  4. Simmer on low for 45 mins. Serve with a dollop of blended fat-free cottage cheese as "sour cream".

🫙 Balsamic Chicken Bowl

Mediterranean vibes with high volume veggies.

Ingredients

  • Shredded chicken
  • 1 can Black beans
  • Diced red onions, celery, cucumbers
  • Sliced black olives (1 pt worth)
  • Balsamic vinegar & squeeze of lime
  • Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken and veggies together.
  2. Dress directly with balsamic vinegar and lime juice.
  3. Add Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper to taste.

🍳 Italian Egg & Potato Hash

Ingredients

  • Bag of fresh yellow potatoes (cubed)
  • 1/2 onion & 1/2 bell pepper (diced)
  • 2-3 eggs per portion
  • Italian seasoning, Salt, pepper, hot sauce

Instructions

  1. Get non-stick skillet hot with 0-pt spray. Saut� potatoes, onions, peppers.
  2. Let moisture evaporate until potatoes get a golden-brown crust. Add seasonings.
  3. Scramble or fry eggs directly into the pan. Finish with hot sauce.

🍔 Pre-Packed Burger Bowls

Ingredients

  • Lean ground beef
  • Garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper
  • 1 head iceberg lettuce (shredded), Diced pickles
  • Secret Sauce: 1 cup fat-free cottage cheese, 4 tbsp low-sugar ketchup, splash of pickle juice, pinch of garlic powder.

Instructions

  1. Brown ground beef with seasonings.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, ketchup, and pickle juice until silky smooth.
  3. Divide shredded iceberg. Top with beef and pickles.
  4. Drizzle "Secret Sauce" over the top.

🍄 Marinated Button Mushroom Salad

Ingredients

  • 8-10 oz button mushrooms, celery, red onion
  • 3 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon
  • Juice of 1/2 Lemon, 1/2 tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Whisk vinegar, dijon, lemon, and spices.
  2. Toss sliced mushrooms and onions into dressing. Marinate 15 mins.

🥩 Heavy-Hitting Beef, Potato & Cabbage Stew

Ingredients

  • Lean beef stew meat cubes
  • Spanish onion, celery, carrots (chopped)
  • Yellow potatoes (cubed), 1/2 head cabbage
  • 1 carton low-sodium beef broth, 2 tbsp Worcestershire

Instructions

  1. Sear beef. Add onion, celery, carrots for 3-4 mins.
  2. Add broth, Worcestershire, spices. Simmer 45 mins.
  3. Stir in potatoes and cabbage. Simmer 30 mins.

🍤 Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Sweet Potato Hash

Ingredients

  • 1.5 - 2 lbs Large shrimp (peeled)
  • 2-3 medium Sweet potatoes (cubed)
  • 2 Bell peppers, 1 Red onion, 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 Lemon, 1 tsp Paprika, 1 tsp Oregano

Instructions

  1. Roast sweet potatoes, peppers, onions at 400F for 20-25 mins.
  2. Toss shrimp with garlic, lemon, oregano.
  3. Add shrimp to pan, roast 5-7 more mins until pink.

🥔 Zero-Point Shepherd's Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 99% fat-free ground turkey
  • 1 bag (16oz) Frozen mixed veg, 1 Onion
  • 1.5 - 2 lbs Yellow/Russet potatoes
  • 1/2 cup Fat-free cottage cheese (blended)
  • 1 cup beef broth, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 1 tbsp Tomato paste

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes. Mash with blended cottage cheese and garlic powder.
  2. Brown turkey and onions. Add broth, paste, worcestershire, veggies. Simmer.
  3. Pour meat to dish, top with potato mash. Bake 375F 20-25 mins.

🥚 Spinach & Tomato Egg Scramble

Ingredients

  • 12 Whole eggs (whisked)
  • 2 Roma tomatoes (diced)
  • 2 handfuls baby spinach
  • Garlic powder, salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Sweat tomatoes in pan until liquid evaporates.
  2. Wilt spinach for 60 seconds. Remove from pan.
  3. Cook eggs low & slow. Fold veggies in before fully set.

🧀 "Melt" Chicken & Fiesta Bean Salad

Ingredients

  • 4 Boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup Fat-free Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan, Spices
  • 1 can Black beans, 1 can Corn, Cherry tomatoes, Red onion, Cilantro, Lime

Instructions

  1. Mix beans, corn, tomatoes, onion, cilantro, lime juice for salad.
  2. Mix yogurt, parmesan, and spices. Smear over chicken breasts.
  3. Bake chicken at 375F for 30-35 mins. Serve over salad.

Weekend Restaurant Guide

Eating out on weekends? Stay on track without ordering a salad and water. These are the lowest-point orders that still feel like a treat at each chain. (Note: Menu items and points are based on Canadian menus for the Ottawa, ON area). Diet pop is always 0 pts — order it without guilt.

🍔

McDonald's

The go-to: McChicken or McNuggets — classic, satisfying, cheaty enough

⭐ The Smart Order (~7–8 pts total)

  • McChicken Sandwich ~7 pts
  • or 6-Piece Chicken McNuggets ~7 pts
  • Apple Slices (swap for fries) 0 pts
  • Diet Coke / Diet Pepsi 0 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • The McChicken is the sweet spot — real chicken, real bun, actual flavour, only 7 pts.
  • McNuggets with Sweet & Sour or BBQ sauce add ~1 pt per packet. Still worth it.
  • Skip the fries entirely or grab apple slices. A small fries is ~9 pts on its own — that's more than the sandwich.
  • A plain Hamburger is also ~7 pts if you want beef instead.
  • Avoid the McDouble and Quarter Pounder — they jump to 12–17 pts fast.
🍔

Wendy's

The power move: Grilled Chicken Sandwich + Small Chili instead of fries

⭐ The Smart Order (~12–13 pts total)

  • Grilled Chicken Sandwich ~8 pts
  • Small Chili (swap for fries) ~4 pts
  • Diet Coke / Diet Pepsi 0 pts
Lighter Option (~9 pts): Jr. Cheeseburger + Small Chili, skip the bun on the chili side. The Jr. is surprisingly filling at only ~9 pts solo.

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Wendy's Small Chili is arguably the best fast food diet swap on the planet — it's a real meal, bean-heavy, and only ~4 pts. Get it instead of fries every time.
  • The Grilled Chicken Sandwich uses real breast meat — not processed. That makes a difference.
  • A plain Baked Potato is ~5 pts and shockingly filling if you want starch.
  • Dave's Single is ~15 pts. Save it for a true cheat day — it's not bad, just not efficient.
🥖

Subway

The most customizable chain — you can engineer a genuinely filling meal for 5–7 pts

⭐ The Smart Order (~5–7 pts total)

  • 6" Turkey Breast on 9-Grain Wheat ~5–6 pts
  • or 6" Rotisserie-Style Chicken on 9-Grain Wheat ~6–7 pts
  • All the veggies you want (lettuce, tomato, onion, cucumber, peppers, spinach) 0 pts
  • Mustard or Vinegar as sauce 0 pts
  • Diet Coke / Diet Pepsi 0 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Subway is the most diet-flexible fast food because you control every component. Use that power.
  • Pile on the free veggies. Double the lettuce and spinach — it bulks up the sub massively for 0 pts.
  • Mustard and vinegar are 0 pts and make the sub taste great. Avoid mayo and ranch — they add 3–5 pts immediately.
  • Skipping cheese saves you ~2 pts. If you need the cheese fix, one slice of provolone is only ~1 pt.
  • A 6" is genuinely filling with enough veggies. Only go 12" if you're very hungry — it doubles the points.
☕

Tim Horton's

Best for breakfast stops and coffee runs — the chili is a hidden gem at any hour

⭐ The Smart Orders

  • Chili (Regular) ~5–6 pts
  • BELT on English Muffin (Bacon Egg Lettuce Tomato) ~7–8 pts
  • Oatmeal with Berries (breakfast) ~5–6 pts
  • Black Coffee or Tea 0 pts
  • Diet Pepsi 0 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Tim's Chili is their best-kept diet secret. It's thick, protein-rich, bean-heavy, and surprisingly filling for ~5–6 pts. Works as lunch or an afternoon stop.
  • The BELT on an English Muffin is the breakfast sweet spot — real ingredients, lower carb base than a bagel, and feels indulgent.
  • Avoid the bagels as a base. A plain bagel alone is ~7 pts before any toppings.
  • Coffee order tip: A splash of 1% milk to cool it down is 0 pts — totally fine. Just skip the Double Double; the cream and sugar in one is ~5 pts on its own.
  • The Turkey Bacon Club wrap is ~10–12 pts — doable but not efficient. Save it for when you're truly hungry.
🥪

Firehouse Subs

Hearty, hot, and satisfying — stick to small sizes with lean meats

⭐ The Smart Order (~10–12 pts total)

  • Small Hook & Ladder® Sub (Fully Involved but NO MAYO) ~10 pts
  • or Small Turkey Bacon Ranch (Lite Ranch) ~12 pts
  • Side Salad (with light Italian/balsamic) 1-2 pts
  • Diet Coke / Pickle Spear 0 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • The Small sub size is all you need. Mediums quickly jump to 20+ points and Larges are massive calorie bombs.
  • "Fully Involved" comes with heavy mayo — ask for it "No Mayo, Mustard only" to immediately knock off 3-4 points.
  • Lean Meats Win: Stick to smoked turkey breast and chicken. Pastrami and meatball subs run extremely high in fat and points.
  • Skip the chips and cookies. Pair the small sub with a side salad or use your own zero-point apple/snack bag. The complimentary pickle spear is 0 points!

KFC

Fried chicken can be tough on points, but you can navigate it

⭐ The Smart Order (~8-10 pts total)

  • 1 pc Original Recipe Chicken Breast (remove skin) ~3-5 pts
  • or 2 Original Recipe Tenders ~4 pts
  • Individual Mashed Potatoes (No Gravy) ~4 pts
  • Diet Pepsi 0 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • The skin and breading hold the vast majority of the fat and points. Peeling the skin off a breast gives you a huge portion of protein for surprisingly low points.
  • Avoid the fries and coleslaw (which is loaded with mayo). Mashed potatoes without gravy are the lowest point side here.

Popeye's Louisiana Kitchen

The trick is getting blackened or stripping the batter

⭐ The Smart Order (~3-8 pts total)

  • Blackened Chicken Tenders (3 pc) ~2-3 pts
  • or 3 Mild Tenders (strip thick batter) ~6 pts
  • Corn on the Cob (No butter) or Green Beans 0-2 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Blackened Tenders are Popeye's best-kept diet secret! They use a spicy seasoning instead of heavy breading. If your location has them, always get them.
  • Their Cajun fries are delicious but completely ruin point budgets (~10+ pts for a small).

Typical Ottawa Shawarma Place

Shawarma Palace, Mangos, 3 Brothers — we live in the Shawarma capital!

⭐ The Smart Order (~6-8 pts total)

  • Chicken Shawarma Salad Bowl ~3-5 pts
  • Veggies (Lettuce, tomato, onion, turnips, pickles, hot peppers) 0 pts
  • Garlic Sauce (Toum) (1 tbsp, on side) ~3 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • The garlic sauce (toum) is essentially whipped oil and garlic. It's high in points, so ask for it on the side and dip your fork into it rather than having it smothered.
  • Skip the rice and potatoes. They are cooked in heavy oil/butter and will cost you 10-15+ points. A salad bowl with chicken provides heavy volume for very few points.
  • Double up on pickled turnips and pickles — they add great acidity and crunch for 0 points!

⭐ The Smart Order (~3-5 pts total)

💡 Know Before You Order

Typical Chipstand / Fry Truck

A summer classic in the Ottawa Valley

⭐ The Smart Order (~10-12 pts total)

  • Standard Hot Dog or Pogos ~10-12 pts
  • or Grilled Chicken Burger (mustard, relish) ~6-8 pts
  • Craving poutine? Split a small fry/poutine 3 ways ~10+ pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Chipstand fries are glorious, but a small fry or poutine can easily cost 25-35+ points. Rule of thumb: grab a hot dog and steal a small handful of fries from whoever you're with.
  • Stick to mustard, ketchup, and relish — they are all virtually 0 points.

Wilno Tavern (Wilno, ON)

World-class Polish comfort food in the Valley!

Typical Canadian-Chinese Food

Golden Palace, local spots, generic takeout

⭐ The Smart Order (~8-10 pts total)

  • Beef or Chicken & Broccoli (sauce drained/light) ~5-7 pts
  • Steamed Rice (1/2 cup portion) ~3 pts
  • Clear Wonton Soup or Egg Drop Soup ~2-3 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Avoid battered and deep fried! General Tso, Sweet & Sour Chicken Balls, and Lemon Chicken run upwards of 20-30 points.
  • Stir fries (like chop suey or mixed vegetables with clean meat) are the hack. Be mindful of thick, glazed sauces and ask them to go "light on sauce" or serve with steamed rice instead of fried rice/noodles.

Sushi Kan (Baseline Road)

AYCE sushi can be completely guilt-free if you order right

⭐ The Smart Order (~2-8 pts total)

  • Sashimi (Shrimp/Ebi, Surf Clam, Scallop) 0 pts!
  • Edamame / Wakame (Seaweed) Salad 0-1 pts
  • Miso Soup ~1-2 pts
  • If craving rolls: 1 Spicy Scallop/Shrimp Roll (6 pc) ~6-8 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Sashimi is completely free. You can eat plates of shrimp and surf clam for 0 points. It's the highest quality protein you can find.
  • Rice is the killer. Sushi rolls are packed with sugary, starchy sushi rice. Limit yourself to one set of Maki rolls, and rely on sashimi, salads, and soups for volume! Avoid anything "Tempura" (deep fried).

Thai Express

Noodles will hurt, but soups and stir-fries save the day

⭐ The Smart Order (~6-10 pts total)

  • Tom Yum Soup (With Chicken/Shrimp) ~3-5 pts
  • Pad Pak Ruam (Stir-fried vegetables & chicken, no rice) ~5-7 pts
  • Mango Salad or Papaya Salad ~5 pts

💡 Know Before You Order

  • Avoid the Pad Thai! It's insanely high in points (sometimes 25+ pts) due to the rice noodles sauteed heavily in oil and sugary tamarind/peanut sauces.
  • Tom Yum soup is incredibly flavourful, spicy, and filling for very few points. A bowl of soup + a side of veggies/chicken is the ultimate cheat code here.
