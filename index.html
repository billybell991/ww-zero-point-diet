Exact volumetric measurements to ensure bold flavors without point blowouts.

🍔 Pre-Packed Burger Bowls (4 Lunches)

A high-volume base mimicking a pub-style cheat meal.

Ingredients

  • Lean ground beef
  • 1 tsp each: Garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper
  • 1 head iceberg lettuce (shredded)
  • Diced pickles
  • Secret Sauce: 1 cup fat-free cottage cheese, 4 tbsp low-sugar ketchup, splash of pickle juice, pinch of garlic powder.

Instructions

  1. Brown ground beef with seasonings. Drain excess fat.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, ketchup, and pickle juice until silky smooth.
  3. Divide shredded iceberg into 4 containers.
  4. Top with beef, pickles, and Marinated Mushroom Salad (see below).
  5. Drizzle "Secret Sauce" over the top.

🍲 Heavy-Hitting Beef, Potato & Cabbage Stew (4 Suppers)

The ultimate overnight metabolic anchor.

Ingredients

  • Lean beef stew meat cubes
  • 1/2 Spanish onion, celery, and carrots (chopped)
  • Yellow potatoes (cubed)
  • 1/2 head of cabbage (chopped into thick ribbons)
  • 1 carton low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1.5 tsp Kosher salt, 1 tsp pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. Sear beef stew cubes with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  2. Add onion, celery, carrots. Cook 3-4 mins.
  3. Add broth and Worcestershire. Simmer covered for 45 mins.
  4. Stir in cubed potatoes and cabbage ribbons.
  5. Simmer another 30 mins until potatoes are tender and cabbage is melted into the gravy.

🥗 Marinated Button Mushroom Salad (Anchor Side)

Ingredients

  • 2 cans whole button mushrooms (drained)
  • 1/2 large Spanish onion (finely chopped)
  • 1/3 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp water + splash of pickle juice
  • 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, pepper, heavy pinch Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. Place mushrooms and onion in a large container.
  2. Whisk vinegar, water, pickle juice, and spices in a small bowl.
  3. Pour marinade over mushrooms, seal, and shake. Marinate in fridge.

🍳 Italian Egg & Potato Hash ("Some Salt" Hash)

Ingredients

  • Bag of fresh yellow potatoes OR frozen 0-point hash browns (cubed)
  • 1/2 onion & 1/2 bell pepper (diced)
  • 2-3 eggs
  • Heavy Italian seasoning, "Some Salt" (1 tsp), pepper, hot sauce

Instructions

  1. Get non-stick skillet hot. Sauté potatoes, onions, peppers (add splash water/cover to steam if needed).
  2. Let moisture evaporate until potatoes get a golden-brown crust. Add seasonings into the pan so they soak in.
  3. Scramble or fry eggs directly into the pan. Finish with hot sauce.
