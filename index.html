Chicken is the star this week — versatile, lean, and deeply satisfying. This rotation hits maximum volume with beans, corn, and tomatoes for almost zero points.

A warm, filling, and perfectly sweet morning classic.

🥗 Southwest Chicken Bowl

A fast, fresh kick of lime and zero-point salsa. Yields: 4 lunches

Ingredients

  • 1 can Black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 can Sweet corn (drained)
  • Fat-free salsa
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. Divide chicken, beans, and corn into 4 prep bowls.
  2. Top generously with fat-free salsa.
  3. Squeeze lime over top and garnish with cilantro.

🍲 Heavy Red Turkey Chili

Rich, thick, and fully zero point. Yields: 4 suppers

Pro Tip: Serve with a dollop of blended fat-free cottage cheese as zero-point "sour cream" — takes this from good to restaurant-quality.
Ingredients
  • 1 lb 99% fat-free ground turkey
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes (or crushed)
  • 2 cans Kidney beans, 1 can Black beans
  • 3 tbsp Chili powder
  • 1 tbsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 cup beef or chicken broth
Instructions
  1. Brown turkey and onions in a large pot.
  2. Add spices; stir until fragrant (1 min).
  3. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth.
  4. Simmer on low for 45 mins, stirring occasionally.

🍳 Italian Egg & Potato Hash

Crispy golden potatoes, fluffy eggs, Italian seasoning — pure comfort food.

Pro Tip: Don't rush the potatoes! Let them sit undisturbed in a hot non-stick pan to develop a real golden-brown crust — that's what makes this feel like a proper restaurant dish.
Ingredients (per supper)
  • Yellow potatoes (cubed — load them up)
  • ½ onion & ½ bell pepper (diced)
  • 2-3 eggs
  • Italian seasoning
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Hot sauce
  • Zero-point non-stick cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Heat non-stick skillet well with cooking spray. Add potatoes, onion, peppers.
  2. Cook until moisture evaporates and potatoes get a golden crust. Don't over-stir.
  3. Season with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  4. Crack eggs directly into the pan. Scramble or fry to preference. Finish with hot sauce.

🥕 Garlic & Herb Ranch-Style Dip

Zero-point savory dip — great with raw carrot sticks, celery, and cucumber. 0 pts

Texture Fix: If nonfat Greek yogurt tastes too tart or chalky straight out of the tub, whisk it vigorously with a fork for 30 seconds with a splash of water or lemon juice. It thins it out slightly and makes it feel like a premium, velvety dip.
Ingredients
  • ½ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp dried dill
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • Raw veggie sticks for dipping (carrots, celery, cucumber)

Shortcut: Stir ½ tbsp Hidden Valley ranch seasoning powder straight into the yogurt instead of the individual spices.

Instructions
  1. Stir all ingredients into Greek yogurt until smooth.
  2. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  3. Serve with raw veggie sticks. Keeps refrigerated for 4–5 days.

🍔 Pre-Packed Burger Bowls

The secret sauce makes these taste completely indulgent. Yields: 4 lunches

Pro Tip: Pack the lettuce, pickles, and sauce separately if you plan to microwave the beef portion at lunchtime.
Ingredients
  • Lean ground beef (enough for 4 bowls)
  • 1 head iceberg lettuce (shredded)
  • 1 jar pickles (diced)
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste

The "Secret Sauce" (1 pt per batch)

  • 1 cup fat-free cottage cheese
  • 4 tbsp low-sugar ketchup
  • Splash of pickle juice
  • Pinch of garlic powder
Instructions
  1. The Sauce: Blend cottage cheese with ketchup, pickle juice, and garlic until silky smooth.
  2. The Beef: Brown ground beef with seasonings. Drain any excess fat.
  3. The Base: Pack shredded iceberg into 4 containers.
  4. Build: Divide beef on top, add pickles, include Mushroom Salad (below).
  5. Finish: Drizzle secret sauce over top (or pack separately).

🍄 Marinated Button Mushroom Salad

Sharp, acidic, crunchy — the perfect side to the burger bowl. Yields: 4 lunch sides

Pro Tip: Marinate the mushrooms and sturdy veggies in the dressing and store together. Keep delicate greens separate — toss them in right at lunchtime.
The Dressing
  • 3 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • Juice of ½ Lemon
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
The Salad
  • 8-10 oz button mushrooms (sliced thin)
  • 2-3 stalks celery (diced)
  • ¼ cup red onion (thin slivers)
  • 4 cups arugula or baby spinach (kept separate)
Instructions
  1. Whisk dressing ingredients together until combined.
  2. Toss mushrooms, celery, and red onion into the dressing. Let sit 15 mins.
  3. Divide marinated mix into 4 containers.
  4. Keep arugula/spinach separate — toss in at lunchtime!

🥩 Heavy-Hitting Beef, Potato & Cabbage Stew

A zero-point pot roast in a bowl. Yields: 4 suppers

Pro Tip: Dropping cabbage straight into the stew is an elite move. It melts into the savory beef broth, soaking up all the flavor and boosting volume for a flat 0 points.
Ingredients
  • Lean beef stew meat cubes (enough for 4 bowls)
  • ½ large Spanish onion (chopped)
  • 1 bag yellow potatoes (cubed)
  • Celery and carrots (sliced)
  • ½ head cabbage (thick ribbons)
  • 1 large carton low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
Instructions
  1. Sear: Brown beef in sprayed pot; season generously with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  2. Aromatics: Add onion, celery, carrots; cook 3-4 mins to soften.
  3. Broth: Add beef broth and Worcestershire. Bring to boil, then simmer covered 45 mins.
  4. Volume: Stir in cubed potatoes and cabbage ribbons.
  5. Finish: Simmer covered 30 more mins until potatoes are fork-tender. Divide into 4 containers.

🍋 Sheet Pan Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Sweet Potato Hash

Roasted, garlicky, done in 30 mins. Yields: 4 lunches

Pro Tip: Stagger the roasting so everything finishes together — sweet potatoes get a 10-min head start, then the peppers and onions join for another 10, then the shrimp go on for the final 5. Sweet potatoes end up with a full 25 mins in the oven. The lemon-garlic marinade can be thin, so don't stress about it coating perfectly — just toss the shrimp in it, then teaspoon-splash any remaining marinade over the shrimp and veggies right before that last 5 mins.
Ingredients
  • 1.5-2 lbs large shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • 2-3 medium sweet potatoes (cubed)
  • 2 bell peppers (any color), chopped
  • 1 small red onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Zero-point cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Sweet Potatoes First: Preheat oven to 400°F. Spread sweet potatoes on a sheet pan, spray lightly, season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Roast 10 mins.
  2. Add the Peppers & Onions: Pull the pan out, push the sweet potatoes to the side, and add the peppers and onions. Spray lightly. Return to oven for another 10 mins. (Sweet potatoes are now 20 mins in.)
  3. Prep Shrimp: While the veggies are in the oven, toss the shrimp with garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  4. Quick Finish: Pull the pan out. Scatter shrimp over the veggies and teaspoon-splash any remaining marinade over everything. Roast 5 more mins until shrimp turn pink and opaque. (Sweet potatoes: 25 mins total.)
  5. Portion: Cool, divide into 4 lunch containers.

🥔 Zero-Point Shepherd's Pie

Real potato mash, real comfort food, zero points. Yields: 4 suppers

Pro Tip: We're making REAL potato mash — not cauliflower. Blending fat-free cottage cheese instead of butter keeps it at zero points while maintaining a rich, silky texture that is genuinely great.
Ingredients
  • 1 lb 99% fat-free ground turkey
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 can whole kernel corn / niblets (drained — not creamed corn, which has added sugar)
  • 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.5-2 lbs yellow or Russet potatoes (peeled, cubed)
  • ½ cup fat-free cottage cheese (blended smooth)
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Dried thyme
Instructions
  1. The Mash: Boil potatoes until fork-tender. Drain, then mash with blended cottage cheese, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, and garlic powder until silky.
  2. The Base: Brown turkey and onion in a sprayed skillet. Add remaining seasonings, tomato paste, Worcestershire, broth, and corn. Simmer until it's not watery.
  3. Assembly: Pour filling into a 9x9 baking dish (or 4 oven-safe containers). Spread potato mash evenly over top.
  4. Bake: 375°F for 20-25 mins until edges bubble and top starts to golden. Cool completely before sealing lids.

🥚 Spinach & Tomato Egg Scramble

Bulk-cooked, zero-point, reheats perfectly. Yields: 4 lunches

Pro Tip: You MUST sweat the moisture out of the tomatoes first — skipping this step turns your prepped scramble into a watery, spongy mess by Day 2. Non-negotiable.
Ingredients
  • 12 whole eggs (whisked well)
  • 2 Roma tomatoes (seeded and diced)
  • 2 large handfuls fresh baby spinach
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Coarse salt and black pepper to taste
  • Zero-point non-stick cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Sweat Tomatoes: Spray skillet, medium-high heat. Sauté tomatoes with a pinch of salt for 3-4 mins until all liquid evaporates.
  2. Wilt Spinach: Reduce heat, add spinach, toss 60 secs until wilted. Remove to a plate.
  3. Low & Slow Eggs: Wipe pan, respray, medium-low heat. Pour eggs, let set slightly, then gently push into soft curds with a spatula.
  4. Fold & Finish: While eggs are still slightly glossy, fold in veggies and seasonings. Remove from heat immediately.
  5. Storage: Cool completely before sealing containers — prevents rubbery eggs.

🧀 "Melt" Chicken & Fiesta Bean Salad

Yogurt-crusted chicken over a bright Tex-Mex bean salad. Yields: 4 suppers

Pro Tip: Using fat-free Greek yogurt as the coating keeps the chicken incredibly moist while baking. The yogurt sets into a savory golden crust — this is the recipe people ask about.
Chicken Ingredients
  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • ½ cup plain fat-free Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp seasoned salt
  • Black pepper to taste
Fiesta Bean Salad
  • 1 can (15oz) black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 can (15oz) sweet corn (drained)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • ¼ cup red onion (finely diced)
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro (chopped)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • ½ tsp cumin
Instructions
  1. The Salad: Combine all salad ingredients, toss with lime juice and cumin. Refrigerate to let flavors mingle.
  2. Prep Chicken: Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a baking dish. Place chicken breasts.
  3. The Coating: Mix yogurt, parmesan, and all spices. Smear evenly over top of each breast.
  4. Bake: Bake uncovered 30-35 mins until cooked through (165°F) and topping is golden.
  5. Portion: Divide salad into 4 containers, top each with a chicken breast.

🧄 Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Chicken (adapted from Life in the Lofthouse)

Pounded thin, marinated in parmesan and lemon, done in 12 minutes. 2 breasts sliced for Greek salad lunches, 2 kept whole for suppers. Yields: 4 breasts

WW Swap: The original recipe uses 1 tbsp olive oil per pound of chicken. Swapping that for zero-point cooking spray brings this to essentially 0 pts per serving — the small amount of parmesan divided across 4 breasts rounds to 0. If you want to keep a tiny drizzle of olive oil for flavour, add 1 tsp to the marinade and it stays under 1 pt per serving.
No Air Fryer? Bake at 400°F for 25 minutes until internal temp reaches 165°F. Works perfectly.
Ingredients (4 breasts)
  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 4 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
  • 4 tbsp white vinegar
  • 4 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Zero-point non-stick cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Pound the chicken: Use a meat mallet or rolling pin to flatten each breast to an even thickness — this is what makes them tender and juicy, not dry.
  2. Make the marinade: In a bowl, mix parmesan, white vinegar, lemon juice, garlic powder, oregano, onion powder, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  3. Coat: Dredge each breast into the marinade, coating both sides thoroughly.
  4. Air fry in batches: Spray air fryer basket with zero-point spray. Cook in a single layer at 375°F for 12 minutes. Do not stack or overlap. Rest 2 minutes before slicing.
  5. Portion: Let all 4 breasts cool. Slice 2 and store for Greek Salad lunches. Keep 2 whole for suppers alongside Roasted Mediterranean Veggies.

🥗 Garlic Parmesan Chicken Greek Salad Bowl

A proper Greek salad — not a diet salad. Big, crunchy, tangy, and loaded with zero-point volume. Yields: 4 lunches

Pro Tip: The dressing is just red wine vinegar, lemon juice, and oregano — all zero points. The olives and optional crumbled feta are the only point drivers. A small amount of each goes a long way here; don't skip them, they're what makes it taste like a real Greek salad.
Salad Ingredients
  • 2 sliced Garlic Parmesan Chicken breasts (from above)
  • 1 large head Romaine lettuce (chopped)
  • 1 English cucumber (diced)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • ½ red onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 green bell pepper (diced)
  • Kalamata olives (~5 per bowl, ~1 pt worth)
  • Optional: Crumbled feta (~1 tbsp per bowl, ~1 pt)
Dressing Options

Option A — Store-bought low-fat Greek (~1-2 pts/serving): Look for a low-fat or light Greek dressing (Renee's Lite, Ken's Lite, etc.). More authentic flavour, worth the point or two.

Option B — Zero-point homemade:

  • 6 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. The Dressing: Whisk red wine vinegar, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper together. Taste and adjust.
  2. Build the base: Divide romaine into 4 containers. Top each with cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and green pepper.
  3. Add olives & feta: Scatter kalamata olives over each bowl. Add a small crumble of feta if using.
  4. Add chicken: Fan sliced garlic parmesan chicken over the top of each bowl.
  5. Dress at lunchtime: Pack the dressing separately and drizzle on right before eating to keep everything crisp.

🫑 Roasted Mediterranean Veggies

A zero-point sheet pan side — roasted until the edges caramelize. Perfect alongside the garlic parmesan chicken. Yields: 4 supper sides

Ingredients
  • 2 zucchini — cut into ½-inch half-moons (halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise)
  • 2 bell peppers (any color) — cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes — whole, left intact
  • 1 red onion — cut into 1-inch wedges through the root (they hold together better than chopping)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Zero-point non-stick cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan (or two) with foil and spray generously with zero-point cooking spray.
  2. Spread veggies in a single layer with space between pieces — no piling or overlapping. Crowded veggies steam instead of roast. Use a second pan if needed.
  3. Group similar veggies loosely together: tomatoes in one corner (they release juice), onion wedges flat-side down, zucchini and peppers spread across the rest.
  4. Spray the tops lightly, then season evenly with garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  5. Roast 20–25 minutes, flipping zucchini and peppers once at the 12-minute mark. Tomatoes and onions can stay put. Done when edges are caramelized and slightly charred.
  6. Divide into 4 containers alongside the 4 whole supper chicken breasts.

Ingredients
  • ½ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp dried dill
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • Raw veggie sticks for dipping (carrots, celery, cucumber)

Shortcut: Stir ½ tbsp Hidden Valley ranch seasoning powder straight into the yogurt instead of the individual spices.

Instructions
  1. Stir all ingredients into Greek yogurt until smooth.
  2. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  3. Serve with raw veggie sticks. Keeps refrigerated for 4–5 days.

🍗 Rotisserie Chicken

Pick one up on Sunday — zero effort, zero cooking, all flavour. Yields: 8 portions (4 lunches + 4 suppers) · 0 pts

Sunday Prep: Grab a rotisserie chicken, strip the breast meat (skinless), and shred or slice it. Half goes cold into the 4 lunch bowls. The other half goes into supper containers alongside the roasted potatoes — reheat in the microwave or air fryer for 3 minutes at 375°F.
What you need
  • 1 Rotisserie Chicken (skinless breast meat only)
Instructions
  1. Strip all the breast meat while the chicken is still warm — it pulls apart easily.
  2. Shred or slice into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Divide into 8 equal portions. 4 go into lunch bowls (served cold). 4 go into supper containers with the roasted potatoes.

🧄 Zero-Point Garlic Sauce (Toum-Style)

The real reason this week exists. Outrageously garlicky and creamy. Goes on everything. 0 pts · Yields: ~1 cup

The key: Traditional toum is an oil-bomb — amazing, but 40+ pts. This version swaps the oil for fat-free Greek yogurt or blended fat-free cottage cheese. You get 95% of the flavour at 0 points. Use fresh garlic cloves — not powder — it's the only non-negotiable here.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup fat-free plain Greek yogurt or 1 cup fat-free cottage cheese (blended smooth)
  • 4–6 cloves fresh garlic, minced or pressed
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp salt (or to taste)
  • Pinch of white pepper (optional)
Optional add-ins
  • ½ tsp cumin (for a deeper Middle Eastern flavour)
  • 1 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely minced
Instructions
  1. If using cottage cheese: Blend it first until completely smooth — a stick blender or small food processor works perfectly.
  2. Combine: Mix yogurt (or blended cottage cheese) with pressed garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Taste and adjust: More garlic for more punch. More lemon for brightness. A pinch more salt rounds it out.
  4. Chill: Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving. The flavour deepens significantly as it sits — overnight is even better.
  5. Use generously on the bowls, the roasted potatoes, or anything else on the plate. Don't be shy with it — it's zero points.

🥙 Shawarma Bowl

All the flavours of a classic shawarma, no bread required. Load it high. Yields: 4 lunches · 0 pts

Your secret weapons: The pickles and pickled beets are what make a shawarma taste like a shawarma — they cut through the richness and add the signature tang. You've already got them. Pack them separately so everything stays crisp until lunchtime.
Per Bowl
  • 1 portion Rotisserie Chicken (shredded breast, from recipe above)
  • Large handful shredded cabbage or romaine lettuce (base)
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup diced cucumber
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced red onion
  • Handful fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • Pickles (already on hand) — packed on the side
  • Pickled beets (already on hand) — packed on the side
  • Big dollop of Zero-Point Garlic Sauce (packed separately)
Assembly
  1. Layer shredded cabbage or romaine as the base in each container.
  2. Top with diced tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  3. Add the cold shawarma chicken on top.
  4. Scatter parsley over everything.
  5. Pack the garlic sauce in a small side container — drizzle on right before eating.
  6. Bring the pickles and pickled beets on the side. They're the payoff.

🥔 Shawarma Roasted Potatoes

Crispy, spiced, zero-point, and built to be buried in garlic sauce. Yields: 4 supper sides · 0 pts

The move: A simple spice dusting on the potatoes is all you need. When you ladle the garlic sauce over top at suppertime, the whole plate comes together — crispy spiced potatoes, tender rotisserie chicken, and a gob of that garlic sauce over everything.
Ingredients
  • 4–5 medium yellow or russet potatoes, cubed (~1 inch)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Zero-point non-stick cooking spray
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with foil and spray generously.
  2. Cube potatoes and spread in a single layer on the pan. Spray tops with cooking spray.
  3. Toss with cumin, paprika, turmeric, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Roast 30–35 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy at the edges.
  5. Divide into 4 containers alongside the 4 shawarma chicken supper portions.
  6. At suppertime: Reheat in the microwave or air fryer (3 mins at 375°F keeps them crispy). Top with a genuinely generous gob of Zero-Point Garlic Sauce.
